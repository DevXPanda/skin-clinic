What We Treat

Featured Treatments Patients Commonly Ask For

Every treatment below begins with a dermatologist consultation — not a fixed package. Parameters, sessions, and expected outcomes are decided after Dr. Harsh Tyagi examines your skin.

Acne Treatment

Clearer skin, treated at the root

Clearer skin starts with understanding the type of acne, its triggers, and the marks it leaves behind before choosing the right medical and procedural plan. Dr. Tyagi assesses hormonal, bacterial, and lifestyle factors together, rather than treating every breakout the same way.

Ideal For
  • Active breakouts & cystic acne
  • Recurring hormonal acne
  • Post-acne marks & oiliness
What It Involves

Skin assessment, topical & oral protocols, peels or laser add-ons where indicated.

Sessions & Downtime

Plan reviewed monthly; most protocols involve minimal to no downtime.

Acne Scar Treatment

Texture, rebuilt in stages

A tailored combination of procedures planned by scar type, depth, and skin recovery goals. Ice-pick, boxcar, and rolling scars respond differently, so the plan is built around what's actually on your skin — not a single fixed resurfacing package.

Ideal For
  • Post-acne pitted scarring
  • Boxcar & rolling scars
  • Uneven skin texture
What It Involves

Combination of fractional resurfacing, subcision, or micro-needling based on scar depth.

Sessions & Downtime

Typically staged over several sessions; downtime varies from none to a few days.

Melasma & Pigmentation

Careful, conservative, consistent

Careful treatment planning for stubborn pigmentation with realistic timelines and emphasis on maintenance. Melasma in particular is treated conservatively to avoid rebound pigmentation — the goal is steady improvement, not an aggressive one-session fix.

Ideal For
  • Melasma & hormonal pigmentation
  • Sun-induced dark patches
  • Post-inflammatory pigmentation
What It Involves

Topical regimens, laser toning or IPL where suitable, strict sun-protection protocol.

Sessions & Downtime

Gradual, multi-session approach; maintenance sessions often advised long-term.

Hair Loss & PRP

Scalp health, evidence-first

Dermatologist-led scalp assessment and evidence-based options for hair fall and thinning. Underlying causes — hormonal, nutritional, or pattern hair loss — are identified first, and PRP is used where the evidence genuinely supports it, not as a default add-on.

Ideal For
  • Pattern hair thinning
  • Diffuse hair fall
  • Early-stage hair loss
What It Involves

Scalp evaluation, bloodwork where needed, topical/oral therapy, PRP sessions if indicated.

Sessions & Downtime

PRP typically spaced monthly for an initial course; no significant downtime.

Book a Hair Consultation →

Laser Hair Reduction

Progressive, skin-type aware

Planned laser sessions for long-term hair reduction with skin-type appropriate settings. Because hair grows in cycles, a course of sessions is required to catch follicles across their active growth phase — not a single-visit result.

Ideal For
  • Unwanted facial & body hair
  • Coarse, resistant hair growth
  • Ingrown-hair prone areas
What It Involves

Diode laser platform with contact cooling, parameters set per skin & hair type.

Sessions & Downtime

Course of sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart; virtually no downtime.

Book a Laser Consultation →

Anti-Aging & Rejuvenation

Subtle, not overdone

Subtle, doctor-led options for lines, texture, skin quality, and facial rejuvenation. The focus is on natural-looking improvement in skin quality and tone — not a visibly "done" result.

Ideal For
  • Fine lines & early aging
  • Dull or uneven skin tone
  • Loss of skin firmness
What It Involves

Skin-quality lasers, photofacials, and targeted rejuvenation protocols.

Sessions & Downtime

Usually staged over a short series; downtime is minimal.

Mole, Wart & Skin Tag Removal

Evaluated first, treated with precision

Office-based removal options after careful medical evaluation of the lesion. Every growth is examined before treatment to rule out anything that needs further investigation, so removal is always a medical decision first.

Ideal For
  • Benign moles & skin tags
  • Warts
  • Raised or irritating lesions
What It Involves

Clinical evaluation, precision removal via CO₂ laser or minor procedure.

Sessions & Downtime

Usually a single session; minimal downtime with proper aftercare.
