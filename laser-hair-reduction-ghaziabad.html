Phoebus Diode Laser Hair Reduction

Dermatologist-supervised diode laser hair reduction with parameters selected according to skin type, hair characteristics, treatment area and clinical assessment.

The Phoebus diode platform is used for progressive reduction of unwanted terminal hair — we use the term "laser hair reduction," not "permanent hair removal." Treatment planning takes into account skin type, hair colour and thickness, treatment area, hormonal factors and previous response. Because hair grows in cycles, a course of sessions is generally required and maintenance may be advised. Contact cooling is used to support epidermal protection and comfort during treatment. Candidacy, preparation, shaving guidance, and expected temporary redness are discussed at consultation. Device wavelength, spot-size and power specifications are shared after confirming the installed model.

Please note: Consultation is required. Results, suitability, sessions, downtime, and risks vary between patients.
