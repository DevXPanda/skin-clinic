Skin Education

Acne Scars

How Acne Scars Are Treated: Options & Timelines

Acne scars are treated based on their type — ice-pick, boxcar, or rolling — using options like microneedling, chemical peels, subcision, or fractional lasers. Timelines vary from a few sessions to several months depending on scar depth and skin recovery.

Pigmentation

Melasma Treatment in Indian Skin: What to Expect

Melasma in Indian skin requires a careful, gradual approach — combining topical treatments, sun protection, and selected procedures. Overly aggressive treatment can worsen pigmentation, so realistic timelines and maintenance are key.

Hair

Hair Fall: Common Causes & When to See a Dermatologist

Hair fall can stem from genetics, hormones, nutritional deficiencies, or stress. Seeing a dermatologist early — especially with sudden or patchy loss — helps identify the cause and start effective treatment sooner.

Laser

Laser Hair Reduction: Sessions, Safety & Results

Laser hair reduction typically needs 6-8 sessions spaced weeks apart, targeting hair in its growth phase. When performed with skin-appropriate settings, it's a safe and effective long-term solution for unwanted hair.
