About Dr. Harsh Tyagi

Dr. Harsh Tyagi is a consultant dermatologist with training in clinical dermatology, dermatosurgery, aesthetic dermatology, and laser procedures. After completing his MBBS from Jawaharlal Nehru Medical College, Belagavi, he pursued his MD in Dermatology, Venereology and Leprosy from SMS&R, Greater Noida. He is currently practicing as Consultant Dermatologist at Ambraja Skin Clinic, RDC and a visiting Consultant Dermatologist at Manipal Hospital, Ghaziabad.


His work spans medical dermatology, hair and scalp disorders, pigmentary concerns, acne and acne scars, dermatosurgical procedures, and selected aesthetic treatments. With a patient-first and evidence-based approach, Dr. Harsh Tyagi aims to provide treatment that is ethical, personalized, and focused on safe, natural-looking outcomes.
