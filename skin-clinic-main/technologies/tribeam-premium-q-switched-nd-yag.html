TriBeam Premium Q-Switched Nd:YAG

A dermatologist-operated laser platform for selected pigmentation concerns, tattoo pigments and individualized laser-toning or rejuvenation protocols, with treatment parameters chosen after clinical assessment.

TriBeam Premium is a Q-switched Nd:YAG laser platform featuring a true flat-top beam profile and multiple handpieces and delivery modes. At Ambraja, it is used only after dermatologist assessment and individualized parameter selection. Depending on the indication, it may be considered for selected pigmentation concerns, tattoo pigments, laser toning and texture or rejuvenation protocols. Suitability, response, sessions, downtime and risks vary between patients.
