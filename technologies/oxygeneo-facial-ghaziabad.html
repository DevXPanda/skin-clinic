OxyGeneo Facial

A customizable, non-invasive facial combining gentle exfoliation, oxygenation from within and the infusion of targeted ingredients, selected according to the patient's skin needs.

OxyGeneo uses a CO2-rich reaction between an OxyPod and treatment gel to create a bubbly surface environment that supports the skin's natural oxygenation response, while gently exfoliating and preparing the skin for targeted ingredients. Depending on the available device configuration and protocol, ultrasound and massage may also be incorporated. Treatment selection follows a skin assessment and the clinic's current OxyPod range. This is a dermatologist-guided facial, not a generic salon facial — built around three core ideas: exfoliation, oxygenation from within, and ingredient infusion/nourishment.

Please note: Consultation is required. Results, suitability, sessions, downtime, and risks vary between patients.
